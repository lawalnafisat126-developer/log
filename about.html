About ONELS

Born in Offa • Serving Nigeria • Built on Trust

Our Story

Founded in the heart of Offa, Kwara State in 2025, ONELS began with one truck and one dream: to connect every corner of Nigeria with fast, reliable, and proud local logistics.

Today we operate a modern fleet across all 36 states, but we never forgot where we came from — Offa runs in our blood.

From local deliveries in Ilorin to heavy haulage to Lagos, Abuja, and Port Harcourt — we deliver excellence, every single time.
